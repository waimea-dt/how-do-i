# Game Asset Libraries

## Game Art and Sprites

- [OpenGameArt.org](https://opengameart.org/)
    - **Recommended**
    - Huge collection of game assets
    - Sprites, backgrounds, tilesets
    - Free and open licences

- [Itch.io Game Assets](https://itch.io/game-assets/free)
    - Free game assets
    - Wide variety of styles
    - Community created

- [Kenney.nl](https://kenney.nl/assets)
    - **Excellent quality**
    - Huge collection of free assets
    - Consistent art style
    - Great for prototyping

- [Craftpix](https://craftpix.net/freebies/)
    - Free game assets
    - 2D sprites and tilesets
    - Good quality


## Game Sound Effects

- [Freesound](https://freesound.org/)
    - **Recommended**
    - Huge sound effect library
    - Community uploaded
    - Free under Creative Commons

- [Zapsplat](https://www.zapsplat.com/)
    - Free sound effects
    - Good quality
    - Easy to search

- [Mixkit](https://mixkit.co/free-sound-effects/)
    - Free sound effects and music
    - No attribution required
    - High quality


## Game Music

- [Incompetech](https://incompetech.com/music/royalty-free/)
    - **Popular choice**
    - Royalty-free music
    - Many genres
    - Free with attribution

- [Purple Planet Music](https://www.purple-planet.com/)
    - Free game music
    - Many styles
    - Easy to use

- [Bensound](https://www.bensound.com/)
    - Royalty-free music
    - Good quality
    - Free with attribution
