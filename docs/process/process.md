# The Design Process

The design process is a structured approach to creating digital and physical products. It helps ensure that solutions are well-planned, user-focused, and meet the needs of the people who will use them.

> [!TIP]
> Use the sidebar to navigate the topics in this guide. If you're new to the design process, start with **Design Process Basics** and work through the sections in order.

## Why Follow a Design Process?

- **User-Focused** - keeps the end-user's needs at the centre of development
- **Systematic** - provides a clear framework to follow from concept to completion
- **Iterative** - allows for testing, feedback, and refinement throughout development
- **Efficient** - reduces wasted effort by planning and testing before full implementation

> [!NOTE]
> The design process applies to software development, web design, product design, and many other creative and technical fields. Different industries may use slightly different models, but the core principles remain the same.
